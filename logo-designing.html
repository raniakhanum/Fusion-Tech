Types Of Logos

Finding the right format for your brand

We'll recommend a direction based on your industry and name: here's what's on the table.

Wordmark

The full business name, styled with distinct custom typography.

Lettermark

Initials, monogram-style, compact and easy to recognize.

Icon / Symbol

A standalone graphic mark that can work independent of the name.

Combination Mark

Icon and wordmark paired together, usable together or apart.

Emblem

Name and symbol contained within a single badge-like shape.

Abstract Mark

A unique geometric form built purely for your brand, tied to no letter.
